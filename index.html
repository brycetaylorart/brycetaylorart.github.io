About

I'm Bryce Taylor, an illustrator and multidisciplinary artist pursuing a BFA in Illustration at Sessions College for Professional Design.

My work combines traditional charcoal drawing, digital illustration, typography, photography, and atmospheric worldbuilding. I enjoy creating immersive visual experiences inspired by dark fantasy, organic forms, texture, and environmental storytelling.
